La Patrona 2 sits at 709 N Main St in Glassboro, an easy walk from campus. The menu runs from tacos al pastor and sopes to tortas, nachos and huevos rancheros.

Chips come with red and green salsa. The guacamole is made to order. Nachos come with chicken or beef. Call ahead and it will be ready when you get here.
