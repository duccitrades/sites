The feature

A salon people stick with

Fancy Nails sits in Suite 6 at 508 Hurffville-Crosskeys Road in Sewell. It's the kind of place where customers know the owners by name and ask for their tech by name too.

Reviews mention Kiki and Steve, the owners, plus Tami, Tony, Carol and Aaron. People come back for the work and stay because everyone is friendly.

Come in for a simple manicure, a full set of acrylics or some nail art, or bring the whole bridal party.

“I have been coming here for over 7 years. The owners, Kiki and Steve are hardworking, kind people.”

Andrea J., on Yelp
