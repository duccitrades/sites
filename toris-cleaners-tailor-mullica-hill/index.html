The shop on Main Street

A family tailor shop, close to home.

Tori's Cleaners & Tailor is a family shop at 214 N Main St that has served South Jersey for over 30 years. You'll also find it on Yelp as Tori's Alterations.

Customers come back for the same reasons: clear communication, honest advice and quality work, often done faster than they expected.

  • Alterations hems, pants, dresses, fit
  • Tailoring suit jackets, dress clothes
  • Dry cleaning drop off at the same counter
