How a visit goes

From drop off to pick up

Five easy stops. You handle the first and the last. We take care of the middle.

  1. Stop 1

    Drop off

    Bring your dog to 259 Fish Pond Road. Tell us what you would like done and anything we should know about your pup.

  2. Stop 2

    Bath

    A good wash for a clean coat. Dogs of every size are welcome in the tub.

  3. Stop 3

    Trim

    A breed haircut shaped to your dog's coat. Customers on Yelp have brought in American Eskimos, Havanese, a Pomchi and three Cavaliers.

    Fluff & finish
