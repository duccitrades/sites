Do I need to fix anything first?

No. I buy houses as-is. No repairs, showings or prep needed.

What do I walk away with?

The agreed price minus your payoffs, like your mortgage. I cover the closing costs, and there is no agent commission.

Are you my real estate agent?

No. I'm the buyer on the contract, acting for myself. I don't represent you the way a licensed agent would, and you don't pay me a commission.

Why might you list the house before closing?

I typically list and sell the house to another buyer before I take title. I only do that with your written okay, and you stay the owner until closing.

Where do you buy houses?

In the Indianapolis, Indiana area. If you're not sure your house is in my area, just ask.

Is there any cost to ask?

No. Reaching out is free and there's no obligation. We talk it through and you decide.
