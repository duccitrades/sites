Good questions

What kind of offer will I get?

A direct offer from us as the buyer. We talk through the exact terms together, so you know what to expect. Nothing vague.

Do I need to fix anything first?

No. We buy houses as-is. Whatever condition it is in is fine.

Are you my real estate agent?

No. We are the buyer on the contract, acting for ourselves. We do not represent you the way a licensed agent would, and you do not pay us a commission.

Who handles the closing?

A title company handles the closing with you, on the date you picked.

What if a buyer isn't lined up by the closing date?

We reach out and either extend the date in writing or cancel the contract. You are never left guessing.
